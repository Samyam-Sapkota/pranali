What this is

Pranali Space is a South Asian platform for exploring different ways of living, learning and relating to our world.

We are not an institution with a syllabus. We are a space — part courtyard, part classroom, part kitchen — where farmers, artists, elders, students, cooks, organisers and wanderers sit at the same table. Indigenous knowledge is not treated as heritage to be preserved behind glass; it is treated as a living technology for the century we are actually in.

Everything we do moves through five elements. They are not decoration. They are how we organise our questions: what we stand on, what we flow with, what we hold, what we burn for, what we breathe.

Pick an element above and the rest of this page will answer in that voice.

The living threads

Nine threads, one weave

  • 01

    Community

    Circles that meet often enough to become obligations — potlucks, repair days, listening rooms, grief circles.

  • 02

    Indigenous knowledge

    Seed keepers, weavers, herbalists and oral historians teaching on their own terms, paid as the experts they are.

  • 03

    Ecology

    Watershed walks, soil work, river mapping — learning a place by the life it can still hold.

  • 04

    Climate justice

    Who pays, who decides, who is asked to move. Organising work grounded in the floodplain, not the conference hall.

  • 05

    Spirituality

    Practice without sales pitch — silence, chant, breath and the long South Asian argument about what a self is.

  • 06

    Food

    Millets, ferments, monsoon cooking and the politics of the plate. We think best with our hands in the dough.

  • 07

    Culture

    Music, film, textile and the printed page — contemporary work that argues with tradition instead of curating it.

  • 08

    Learning

    Slow cohorts, apprenticeships and reading groups. No certificates. Attendance measured in conversations.

  • 09

    Retreats

    Weeks away in the hills and the delta, where the schedule is thin enough for something to actually change.
