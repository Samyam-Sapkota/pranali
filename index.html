What this is

Pranali Space is a South Asian platform for exploring different ways of living, learning and relating to our world.

We are not an institution with a syllabus. We are a space — part courtyard, part classroom, part kitchen — where farmers, artists, elders, students, cooks, organisers and wanderers sit at the same table. Indigenous knowledge is not treated as heritage to be preserved behind glass; it is treated as a living technology for the century we are actually in.

Everything we do moves through five elements. They are not decoration. They are how we organise our questions: what we stand on, what we flow with, what we hold, what we burn for, what we breathe.

Pick an element above and the rest of this page will answer in that voice.
