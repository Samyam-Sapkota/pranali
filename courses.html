        Reduced rate

        If the price is the thing stopping you, write to us

        We hold a number of reduced-price and free memberships each year for students, growers, community organisers and anyone for whom the standard price is genuinely out of reach.

        There is no form, no means test and no automated decision — you write a couple of honest lines to a person, and a person writes back. We do not ask for documentation and we do not publish who receives one.
